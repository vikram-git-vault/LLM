I bought the NovaBuds Pro wireless earbuds three months ago, mainly for
commuting and video calls. The sound quality is genuinely good for the
price, and the noise cancellation handles train noise better than I
expected.

The problems started in week four. The left earbud now disconnects
roughly every twenty minutes and I have to put it back in the case to
re-pair it. Battery life has also dropped noticeably. It used to last a
full working day, now I am charging by mid-afternoon. The companion app
crashes on launch about half the time on Android, and the firmware
update it keeps prompting me for has failed four times in a row.

I contacted support two weeks ago and received an automated reply, but
nothing since. For a product at this price point I expected either
better reliability or faster help. I would still recommend the sound,
but not the software or the support.

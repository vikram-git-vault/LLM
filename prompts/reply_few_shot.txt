Write a customer-support reply in the style shown in the examples.

The reply must:
- Thank the customer briefly
- Name the specific problem they reported
- Offer one concrete next step
- Stay under 80 words
- Avoid apologising more than once
- Avoid promises that are not in the examples
- Return ONLY the reply text

Example 1:

Input:
battery died after two weeks and support never replied.

Output:
Thank you for flagging this. A battery failing within two weeks is not
expected behaviour, and your earlier message should not have gone
unanswered. I have escalated your case for a replacement unit. You will
receive a shipping label within two working days.


Example 2:

Input:
great screen but the app crashes constantly on android.

Output:
Thank you for the detailed feedback. Repeated crashes on Android point
to a known compatibility issue rather than a fault with your device.
Our engineering team is tracking it, and I have added your report to
the ticket so you are notified when the fix ships.


Example 3:

Input:
i was charged twice and nobody will help me.

Output:
Thank you for raising this. A duplicate charge should be reversed
automatically, and it clearly was not. I have submitted a refund request
for the second charge. Refunds usually appear within five working days,
and I will confirm once it is processed.
